How We Began

Revolutionizing the Grocery Experience Since 2021

FreshCart was founded with a singular, passionate mission: to solve the paradox of modern grocery shopping. While cities had access to mass-market supermarkets, produce was often weeks old, treated with synthetic waxes, and stripped of its natural flavor.

We set out to create a direct-to-consumer farm network. By establishing temperature-monitored collection hubs near agricultural belts across Himachal Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu, we reduced harvest-to-table transit times from 5 days to under 12 hours.

Today, over 25,000 households across Bengaluru trust FreshCart for their morning cow milk, organic greens, sourdough breads, and everyday kitchen essentials.
