Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Quick answers to common questions about our delivery, returns, and organic certifications.

How does the 2-Hour Express Delivery work?
Our climate-controlled fulfillment micro-hubs are strategically located within 5 km of every serviceable neighborhood. When your order is placed, automated pickers pack your items in under 15 minutes, and our cold-chain delivery riders dispatch directly to your address.
Are all vegetables and fruits 100% organic?
Yes! All our fresh produce is sourced from certified organic farms tested in accordance with NPOP (National Programme for Organic Production) standards. We never use carbide ripening agents, synthetic wax coatings, or harmful chemical preservatives.
What happens if an item arrives damaged or not fresh?
We adhere to a strict "No Questions Asked" doorstep return policy. You can inspect your produce upon delivery and hand back any item you are not thrilled with to the delivery agent. The refund is credited back immediately.
What payment methods do you support?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), all major Credit & Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and Internet Banking.
